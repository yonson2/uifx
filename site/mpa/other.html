After Stuart Langridge's View transitions for changing themes.

Here a theme switch is a page load, as in the original: a cross-document view transition. The Light and Dark links load this page again in that theme, so the scroll position goes back to the top. The last link loads the first page in the other theme. The page links in the header keep the theme and get no transition. Browsers without cross-document view transitions load the page without the wave.
